What changed this round:
0. NEW: the "AI is paused" notice now appears ONCE, when the chat is
   handed to a human. Further messages you send while waiting no longer
   repeat the banner - they are still stored and shown to the human agent
   in the console (and forwarded to Intercom when connected).
1. The console now shows new customer messages while the AI is paused -
   the open conversation refreshes itself every 5 seconds. Your half-typed
   reply is never touched by the refresh, and messages you already sent
   stay in view.
2. Sources are pinned to THIS conversation's brand. The brand is detected
   once per conversation (from the message or the voucher image) and sticks
   - generic follow-up words cannot drop it, and another brand can only
   take over if the customer explicitly names it. The console's SOURCES
   USED list is scoped the same way (this was the IKEA/Max Fashion bug).
3. The voucher-image answer quotes the real error line, never the
   template's field label ("ERROR MESSAGE" is skipped, "VOUCHER CODE IS
   INVALID" is picked), and "how do I redeem" always includes the brand's
   redeem chunks.
